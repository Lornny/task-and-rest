Privacy Policy — Task and Rest

Last updated: August 2026

Task and Rest is a Chrome extension that provides a 5-phase focus timer. This page explains what data the extension does and does not collect.

Data collection

Task and Rest does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. It does not use analytics, tracking pixels, or any remote server.

Everything stays on your device.
All data the extension creates — your task name, session goal, timer settings, session history, and notes — is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage.local API. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server operated by the developer or any third party.

What the extension does NOT do

  • Does not collect personally identifiable information (name, email, address, etc.)
  • Does not track your browsing history or activity on other websites
  • Does not use cookies or third-party analytics
  • Does not require an account, login, or authentication of any kind
  • Does not sell or share data with third parties

Permissions used

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions, each used strictly for its core function:

  • storage — to save your session state and history locally on your device
  • alarms — to keep the timer running accurately in the background
  • notifications — to notify you when a work phase ends
  • sidePanel — to display the extension's interface in Chrome's side panel

External links

The extension includes an optional "Buy me a koffee" link that opens ko-fi.com in a new browser tab. This is a standard external hyperlink — clicking it takes you to Ko-fi's website, which has its own separate privacy policy. The extension itself does not share any data with Ko-fi.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date.
